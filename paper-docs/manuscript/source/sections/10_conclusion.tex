\section{Conclusion}

This study gives a split answer to the question of encrypted DNAGPT inference. At the demonstrated
scope, client-assisted CKKS reproduces the independently validated plaintext computation with
$4.64\times10^{-9}$ relative error against a tolerance of $4\times10^{-2}$ fixed before the run.
Correctness is therefore not the barrier. Memory did prevent chained evaluation in the evaluated
non-interactive configuration. Explicit re-encryption moves that accelerator-memory barrier by
resetting the depth budget at declared boundaries, and the resulting client-assisted process peaks
at $9.6$~GiB of GPU memory. Bounded plaintext caching addresses a separate host-memory problem by
releasing encoded templates at stage boundaries. The feasibility verdict is affirmative at this
scope.

Cost remains the unanswered deployment constraint. The optimization campaign removes repeated host
encoding while preserving the checked operation schedule, packing, multiplicative depth, and
reference. The server still evaluates the released block's dense maps under CKKS, and the data
owner still performs the declared exact nonlinearities. These invariants make a future paired
timing comparison interpretable, but the committed evidence does not yet support an elapsed-time
claim for the retained implementation. Feasibility and practicality consequently have different
verdicts: the encrypted path is correct and fits the evaluated memory envelope, while its service
cost remains unresolved.

\subsection*{Future work}

The immediate measurement priority is a repeated, paired dedicated-node comparison of the retained
implementation and its documented baseline. The next arithmetic milestone is to execute the built
full classifier with its released head at task length and compare the encrypted prediction with the
frozen reference. Systems work should then cache mask plaintexts, extending the encode-once strategy
already validated for weight diagonals. Distinct transforms across the packed activation copies are
the larger circuit opportunity; before mask and copy-repair overhead, the ledger projects a $60\%$
reduction in dense ciphertext--plaintext work. A thread-safe cryptographic context would permit
concurrent independent client boundaries. Protocol closure also requires a noise-flooding budget
for re-encryption. A deployment study must finally add serialized network transport and private
token-index lookup before measuring the complete service.

\section*{Ethics statement}

This study involved no human participants, specimens, or private clinical records. It used public
reference datasets with recorded provenance. The work makes no clinical-performance claim and
does not present the prototype as a deployable privacy guarantee beyond its stated threat model.

\section*{Data availability statement}

All evaluation datasets are public. Their original sources, versions, preprocessing, and any
recovery route used are recorded in the repository's data-provenance document. Sequence data,
model weights, and cryptographic keys are not redistributed.

reuse license has yet been assigned.
