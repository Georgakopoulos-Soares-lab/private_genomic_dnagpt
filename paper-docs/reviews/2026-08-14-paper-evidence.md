# Evidence audit — 2026-08-14

Scope: the complete manuscript, all generated figures, the four evidence ledgers, the implementation
ground-truth note, and the primary artifact for the 2026-08-12 complete-model execution. The audit
also checked the repository documents that `AGENTS.md` and `paper-docs/AGENTS.md` designate as sources
of truth.

The system-Python invocation of `check_numbers.py` could not import PyYAML. The supported command
`paper-docs/scripts/build.sh lint` ran in the paper virtual environment and reported banned terms,
numbers, citations, and structure all clean. The findings below are the arithmetic, dtype, scope,
tag, and cross-artifact defects that the permissive literal-number check does not detect.

## Verified clean

- The 2026-08-12 complete-model artifact supports the central feasibility result: all twelve released
  blocks and the task head executed at 103 tokens; the decrypted label is `N`; the float64 manual and
  float32 full-prompt references also both label the input `N`; no homomorphic bootstrap was used.
- All complete-model timings and primary derived values recompute: `6683 / 3600 = 1.8564 h`;
  server plus client equals `6594.748 s` encrypted evaluation; their shares are `85.5%` and `14.5%`;
  the uninstrumented remainder is `59.504 s`; the within-run block mean is `547.166 s`; the observed
  block-time spread is `4.70%`; and `956.187 / 10,299 = 0.09284 s` per physical crossing.
- The complete-model run is consistently presented as one sample, not a mean, service rate, or
  run-to-run variance estimate. The manuscript explicitly withholds network latency and encrypted
  task-set accuracy.
- Contended single-block timings do not enter the manuscript. The shared-host values `663`,
  `652.545`, `531.933`, `120.612`, and their timing shares are absent. The single-block source is used
  only for correctness, operation counts, depth, and process memory, which contention cannot change.
- The open-provenance `14 GB`, `10%`, `7560 s`, `2.1 h`, and `11.6x` claims do not enter the manuscript.
- The checked one-block operation schedule is consistent throughout: 156 dense products, 177,734
  ciphertext--plaintext multiplications, 1,506 ciphertext--ciphertext multiplications, 8,173
  rotations, 8,776 reductions, 857 physical crossings, and 129,162 logical values. The `7.92x`
  packing factor is correctly called an operation-count reduction, never a speedup.
- Derived circuit arithmetic is correct: softmax carries `128,544 / 129,162 = 99.52%` of logical
  boundary values; attention accounts for `(727+727)/1506 = 96.55%` of ciphertext--ciphertext
  multiplications; 103 tokens require 13 groups and 91 causal tiles; 1,755 tokens require 220 groups
  and 24,310 causal tiles.
- Depth 13 is described as the smallest demonstrated passing depth and tied to level-0 client
  re-encryption, not to a speed advantage. The 80 GB non-interactive memory result is scoped to the
  evaluated backend, packing, parameters, and hardware rather than presented as an impossibility
  theorem.
- The encrypted boundary is stated correctly in the prose abstract, threat model, results, and
  limitations: token-index lookup remains client-side, and the measured client/server roles are
  in-process rather than networked. The graphical-abstract exception is listed above.
