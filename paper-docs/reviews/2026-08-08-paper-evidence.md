## Verified clean

- The mechanical checker is clean under `paper-docs/.venv-paper/bin/python`.
- The two-stage verification chain is stated in Sections 4 and 7: every upstream block and the head
  are checked at `2e-5`, then the encrypted block is checked at `4.64e-9` against a pre-declared
  `4e-2` tolerance. The tolerance/error ratio is about `8.62e6`, which supports "seven orders of
  magnitude."
- All checked operation counts agree with the implementation-ground-truth re-derivation: 156 dense
  products, 177,734 ciphertext--plaintext multiplications, 1,506 ciphertext--ciphertext
  multiplications, 8,173 rotations, 8,776 reductions, 857 physical crossings, and 129,162 logical
  values. `1236 / 156 = 7.923`, so 7.92x is correctly described as an operation-count reduction and
  never as a speedup.
- The boundary decomposition recomputes: 128,544 softmax values out of 129,162 total is 99.52%,
  displayed as 99.5%; 120.612 s divided by 857 is 0.14074 s, displayed as the derived 0.141 s.
- Packing and cryptographic parameters are consistent: four copies by 1,024 features by eight lanes
  gives 32,768 slots; 103 tokens require 13 groups with one padded lane; depth 13 is described as the
  smallest demonstrated passing depth, and level-0 re-encryption is correctly tied to the reset
  mechanism rather than to speed.
- The non-interactive 80 GB result is scoped to the evaluated backend, parameters, packing, and
  hardware and is not presented as a general impossibility result.
- Composition evidence is honest: two blocks at two tokens are measured at `8.48e-11`, while the
  twelve-block task-length run is marked unexecuted and encrypted task accuracy is not claimed.
- Whole-model quantities in manuscript prose are called projections at fixed circuit, apart from the
  figure wording identified above. Section 8 states the omitted task head, inter-block refresh cost,
  and token-index lookup.
- The plaintext baseline now uses DNAGPT's 0.9151 signal-recognition reference and explicitly marks
  the split mismatch. No DeepGSR value is misattributed in the current table or prose.
- Model confidentiality is disclaimed plainly in the threat model and limitations. The encrypted
  boundary begins at embedded vectors, client/server calls are identified as in-process, and no
  networked latency or encrypted final prediction is claimed in manuscript prose.
- The unresolved `client.share_latest = 10%` value does not appear in the manuscript.
